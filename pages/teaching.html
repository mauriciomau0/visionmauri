How I teach

5 methods
Methods

The methods I rely on in the classroom:

  • Problem-based learning: classes begin with a meaningful question.
  • Live coding: models are built in front of the class, including mistakes, so debugging becomes part of learning.
  • Scaffolding: support is gradually reduced as students gain autonomy.
  • Active recall: students retrieve concepts instead of rereading them.
  • Formative assessment: low-stakes checks show what needs reteaching.
Approach

My classrooms bring together students from different fields and levels of experience with math and code. I start with a real problem rather than a formula, giving everyone a way into the material. Through scaffolding and feedback, students build toward shared understanding from different starting points.

This matters because AI is changing how people relate to work, learning, and purpose. When students disengage, it is often because they cannot see why the work matters. A strong classroom gives them a problem worth solving, a question worth answering, and recognition for the effort they make. I am not trying to outrun AI. I want to create a room people still have a reason to enter.
